How Gen 3 damage works

Each damaging move has one attack type. For a Pokémon with two defending types, the move gets a multiplier against each type. Multiply those numbers together, even if one is 0.

In Gen 3, whether a damaging move uses Attack/Defense or Sp. Atk/Sp. Def is decided by the move's type, not by the individual move. This calculator identifies that category. Status moves do not deal direct damage.

This shows type effectiveness, not exact HP damage. Exact damage also depends on move power, stats, level, random variation, weather, abilities, held items and other battle effects. Abilities like Levitate can override type outcomes.
